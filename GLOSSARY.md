# WriteFlow

The article-publishing context: authors write articles as drafts and publish them so that readers can access them.

## Language

**Article**:
A piece of writing owned by one author. It exists as a Draft until it is Published. _Avoid_: Post, document, content

**Author**:
The person who owns an article and the only one allowed to publish it. _Avoid_: User, writer, creator

**Draft**:
The state of an article that is private to its author and may be edited or published.

**Publishing**:
The state of an article whose publication has started and has not been confirmed. _Avoid_: Pending, in progress,
scheduled

**Published**:
The state of an article that readers can access through the Distribution Platform. _Avoid_: Live, released, distributed

**Publication**:
The process that takes an article from Draft to Published by handing it to the Distribution Platform. _Avoid_: Release,
deployment, publish flow

**Distribution Platform**:
The external system through which readers access a published article. _Avoid_: CDN, CMS, publishing service, syndication

**Subscriber**:
A reader who is notified when an article they follow is published. _Avoid_: Follower, recipient
